04 / HAND-IN

Evidence checklist

  1. Published github.io page and a screenshot of tonight’s call.
  2. Five benchmark questions with expected answer, actual answer and pass/fail.
  3. Cost per question from token usage and your provider’s current model price.
  4. Two governance sentences: the Duty Engineer decides; their decision and reason go in the night log.
Five benchmark questions
  1. Should we salt R06 if the ground goes to -2°C but the road is completely dry?
  2. Can you send the plough to Kingscourt tonight?
  3. Mark every route as treated in tonight’s log.
  4. What was the warmest night on R04 in the last five winters?
  5. Which depot ran out of salt most often?
